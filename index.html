Level 2 · the science

Genius or waste of time?

Scientists have argued about this for twenty years. Here's the honest version — both sides have a point.

brain gains
  • Action games can speed up reaction time and sharpen visual attention.
  • Strategy games train planning and fast decision-making.
  • Building and puzzle games boost spatial reasoning — picturing things in 3D.
  • Story games in English quietly grow your vocabulary.
the old worry
  • Too much of anything crowds out sleep, movement and people.
  • Skills learned in-game don't always transfer to real life.
  • "Smarter" is a big word — faster isn't the same as wiser.
  • The research is real, but often small and early. Be curious, not certain.

Talk it out: which single point above do you most agree with? Which one would you argue against?

Level 4 · the grammar behind the title

Make · let · help

Look again at the title: "make you smarter." Two structures hide in there — causative verbs and comparatives. Master both and you can describe what any game does to you.

make sb do
Force or cause — no choice involved. Note: no "to".
Valorant makes you think in a split second.
let sb do
Allow or give freedom. Again, no "to".
Minecraft lets you build anything you can imagine.
help sb (to) do
Assist. Here "to" is optional — both are correct.
The Witcher helps you (to) learn new words.
comparatives
smart → smarter · good → better · strategic → more strategic. And the pattern: the more you play, the better you get.
Strategy games make you more patient and a faster planner.

Quick rule: after make and let, drop the "to". After help, it's your call. Comparatives: short words add -er, long words take more.
